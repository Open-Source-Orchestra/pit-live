      • ⟠ Ethereum

        A world computer nobody owns

        Ethereum is a decentralized blockchain network and software platform. Since 2015 it has grown into a home for apps that run 24/7 for anyone with an internet connection — and no single person or company runs it.

        Source: ethereum.org · What is Ethereum?

      • ⟠ Ethereum · 🌍 our planet

        Light on the planet

        Since the Merge in September 2022, Ethereum runs on proof-of-stake. ethereum.org quotes the CCRI estimate: the Merge cut Ethereum’s yearly electricity use by more than 99.988%.

        Source: ethereum.org · energy consumption

      • ⟠ Ethereum · 🎻 music

        Tips straight to the artist

        Fork the First Artist (built at ETHGlobal Mumbai, November 2026) lets a fan’s own wallet send USDC or ETH to an artist’s ENS name: 96.31% to the artist, 3.69% to their AI steward’s compute, 0% to the Open Source Orchestra. Nobody holds anyone’s money — and a sent transfer is final.

        More: artist.opensourceorchestra.org

      • 🎻 ʻohana music

        The orchestra is whoever shows up

        The Open Source Orchestra is a circle of musicians who play open rooms at Ethereum gatherings: no auditions, no gatekeepers, no wrong instrument. From Devcon 6 in Bogotá and Devcon SEA in Bangkok to the Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary in Mumbai.

        More: opensourceorchestra.org

      • 🎻 ʻohana music

        Four days in one hall

        The Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary, Jio World Centre, Mumbai, 3–6 November 2026: cane stools in a circle, drums on rugs, a mandala of light on the wall. About 35 artists took turns at the mics; on the last evening the room held Devcon 8’s closing ceremony.

        See it: the Mandala Room · 35 photos

      • 🦋 butterflies

        Butterflies pollinate too

        By day they visit many wildflowers, probing for nectar — their flight fuel. They move pollen less efficiently than bees, the US Forest Service notes, but they carry it from flower to flower all the same.

        Source: US Forest Service · butterfly pollination

      • 🌍 our shared planet

        Pollinators feed us

        Nearly 90% of wild flowering plants depend, at least in part, on animals to move their pollen — and more than three quarters of the leading types of global food crops rely on animal pollination to some extent.

        Source: IPBES pollinators assessment (2016), key messages

      • 🌱 the infinite garden

        A garden, not a game to win

        The Ethereum Foundation’s picture of Ethereum is an infinite garden: coordination tended like a garden, so that it may continue to thrive. In its words: “nurture and grow, but do not control, and continue to play.”

        Source: ethereum.foundation · Nurturing the Infinite Garden

      • 🕳️ the PIT

        The floor, not the stage

        This radio carries a mixer feed, not a camera on faces. Recording needs consent: it is announced at the mixer, anyone can ask not to be on the feed, and only what was consented is archived.

        The standard: PIT Standard V2

      • 🪕 the ukulele’s road

        From Madeira to Hawaiʻi

        The ukulele comes from Portugal: the little machete of Madeira sailed to Hawaiʻi on the Ravenscrag in 1879. Kala ukuleles travel with Shaka and are put in strangers’ hands — Japan, China, India, Thailand.
